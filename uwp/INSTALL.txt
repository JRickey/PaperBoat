Paper Boat for Xbox Developer Mode (US ROM only)

1. Install the signed MSIX bundle and its certificate through Xbox Device Portal.
2. Generate pm64.o2r from a legally dumped US Paper Mario ROM.
3. Copy pm64.o2r to E:\PaperBoat\pm64.o2r on the Xbox USB drive.
4. Launch Paper Boat. Press the Xbox View/Back button to open the port menu.

Writable configuration and saves use the app's LocalState. Optional mods and
paperboat-hd.o2r can be placed under E:\PaperBoat.
